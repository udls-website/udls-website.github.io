📖 About untether

untether is a lightweight app that lives in your menu bar or system tray and lets you instantly turn on your Android phone's Wi-Fi hotspot over Bluetooth Low Energy. No more digging through phone settings or typing in passwords — just a single click on your computer and your phone starts sharing its connection.

Built with Go on the desktop side and Jetpack Compose with Material 3 on Android, untether is designed to be fast, reliable, and easy enough for anyone to use. It works without root by leveraging Shizuku, a popular tool that grants apps elevated permissions safely and securely.

Whether you're tethering on the go, sharing your connection during a meeting, or just want a quicker way to get online, untether makes the process seamless. This guide will walk you through downloading and running the application on Windows in just a few minutes.

✨ Key Features

  • Instant hotspot activation — Turn on your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot directly from your computer with a single click.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy — Uses efficient, low-power Bluetooth to communicate with your phone, saving battery on both devices.
  • No root required — Works with Shizuku to grant the necessary permissions without modifying your phone's system.
  • Menu bar convenience — Always accessible from your system tray or menu bar, so you never have to open a full app window.
  • Modern Android app — Built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 for a clean, native experience on your phone.
  • Cross-platform design — The desktop component is written in Go, making it portable and lightweight.

⚙️ How It Works

untether uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to send a command from your computer to your Android phone. On the phone, a companion app listens for these commands and, with the help of Shizuku, toggles the Wi-Fi hotspot on or off. Because Shizuku handles the system-level permission, you don't need to root your phone or go through complex setup steps.

Here's what happens behind the scenes: You install the untether app on your phone (available from the same releases page), grant it Shizuku access, and pair it with your computer once. After that, every time you want to share your connection, just click the untether icon in your system tray and select your phone. The hotspot turns on within seconds, and you're ready to connect other devices.

The desktop app is built in Go and runs quietly in the background. It uses the Bluetooth stack of your operating system to discover and communicate with your phone. No additional drivers or complex configuration are needed.

Turning it off works exactly the same way, just in reverse.

      Because the message travels over Bluetooth rather than your mobile data or a Wi-Fi network, it still works when your phone has no data connection of its own, and it works even if your phone's screen is off. You never have to unlock anything to send the command.

After that, every future hotspot session is a single click away. The apps remember each other, so you do not have to repeat the pairing process each time you reboot.

      • Shizuku keeps stopping. Shizuku needs to be restarted after a phone reboot unless you have set it up to start automatically. Open Shizuku, start it again, then return to untether.

      Do I need to root my Android phone?
      No. untether relies on Shizuku to obtain the access it needs, which means your phone's system software stays untouched.

      Does this use my mobile data to send commands?
      No. The commands travel over Bluetooth Low Energy between your computer and your phone, so they do not consume mobile data.

      Will it drain my battery?
      Bluetooth Low Energy is designed for low power use, and the app only communicates when you trigger an action. Turning the hotspot off when you are done has a far bigger effect on battery life.

      Can I control more than one phone?
      Yes. Pair each phone you want to control, and choose between them from the untether menu on your computer.

      Do I need to keep the desktop app open?
      You should leave it running in the background, since that is what lets it respond to your clicks from the tray. It uses very little memory.

      How do I stop a phone from being controlled?
      Remove it from the untether menu on your computer, or revoke Shizuku permission in the phone app. Either one ends the arrangement immediately.
